# AgriLoss screen-recording plan

**Target length:** about 3 minutes; maximum 3 minutes.  
**Output:** `.webm` or `.gif`, saved under `demo/`.  
**App:** <https://agriloss-rwanda.streamlit.app/>

Record the browser screen with a desktop recorder. Keep the pointer visible. Do not use a phone to record a desktop screen. Show the deployed app, not a slide deck.

## Timed run of show

| Time | Screen action | Suggested narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Title, release notice, Start here sample indicators. | “AgriLoss shows unweighted SAS 2024 sample summaries for storage and value addition. These are not national estimates.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Crop workspace, choose storage, show the investigation queue and change the sort rule. | “The queue flags crop groups at or above the named-crop upper quartile for reported positive storage or transport loss. The denominator and comparison cutoff are shown; this is a relative screen, not an investment ranking.” |
| 0:45–1:15 | Select a crop, enter the cooperative or market area and an optional planning season, choose local conditions, and submit for recommendation review. | “The crop recommendation is based on SAS evidence. The location and selected conditions tailor field actions; they do not filter the survey data.” |
| 1:15–1:35 | Review the local action list, expand the report preview, then download and open the report. | “The report keeps crop evidence, province context, user-entered conditions, and required checks in separate sections.” |
| 1:35–1:55 | Switch to processing and value addition; show a crop/use option, reported-use counts, sales evidence, and a Rwanda example. | “These routes are tied to food, drink, or other transformation reported in the sample. Rwanda examples show a process has been documented; buyers, current prices, yield, cost, and feasibility still need local validation.” |
| 1:55–2:20 | Open Production links; show a crop practice comparison and crop-by-market-channel price view. | “Season A practice and input responses are joined to production after plot and weight checks. The reported prices are historical survey values, not current quotes, and the associations are not causal.” |
| 2:20–2:40 | Explore the sample; change a sidebar filter. | “Season and province charts remain separate from crop summaries. Season C is a restricted sample.” |
| 2:40–3:00 | Data and methods, then stop on the title. | “The classifier checks a past Season A to Season B response; it does not forecast future loss. This workflow supports investigation, not investment decisions or impact claims.” |

## Before recording

- Wait for Streamlit to finish loading and close unrelated tabs.
- Keep the sidebar expanded and start on **Start here**.
- Leave the provisional-release notice visible long enough to read.
- Keep the recording under three minutes.
