# AgriLoss screen-recording plan

**Target length:** about 2 minutes 30 seconds; maximum 3 minutes.  
**Output:** `.webm` or `.gif`, saved under `demo/`.  
**App:** <https://agriloss-rwanda.streamlit.app/>

Record the browser screen with a desktop recorder. Keep the pointer visible. Do not use a phone to record a desktop screen. Show the deployed app, not a slide deck.

## Timed run of show

| Time | Screen action | Suggested narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Title, release notice, Start here sample indicators. | “AgriLoss shows unweighted SAS 2024 sample summaries for storage and value addition. These are not national estimates.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Crop workspace, choose storage, change the sort rule. | “Each sort uses one measure. The selected crop’s rank, valid denominator, and observed data-quality checks are shown.” |
| 0:45–1:15 | Select a crop and province context, enter a local condition, read evidence checks. | “Province figures are separate context, not a crop-by-province result. The local condition adds a question to verify; it does not change survey evidence.” |
| 1:15–1:35 | Download and open the follow-up brief. | “The brief records the measure, sample evidence, local context, and checks to complete before planning.” |
| 1:35–2:00 | Switch to processing and value addition; show sales and price-quality metrics. | “We surface zero and missing prices beside positive sales, and direct users to verify current market data, yields, and costs locally.” |
| 2:00–2:20 | Explore the sample; change a sidebar filter. | “Season and province charts remain separate from crop summaries. Season C is a restricted sample.” |
| 2:20–2:30 | Data and methods, then stop on the title. | “The classifier is retrospective, not a forecast. The workflow supports investigation, not investment decisions or impact claims.” |

## Before recording

- Wait for Streamlit to finish loading and close unrelated tabs.
- Keep the sidebar expanded and start on **Start here**.
- Leave the provisional-release notice visible long enough to read.
- Keep the recording under three minutes.
