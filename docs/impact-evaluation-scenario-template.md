# Decision-support study scenario card

Prepare one card per scenario. Use the same card for the source-based and AgriLoss conditions. Fill it from the published aggregate and the current dashboard, then check every displayed value and denominator before the session. Do not add respondent-level or identifying information.

## Facilitator setup

- Scenario ID: `[A or B]`
- Challenge track: `[storage/collection or processing/value addition]`
- Crop group: `[published crop name]`
- Planning location: `[fictional district/cooperative/market area]`
- Planning period: `[fictional period]`
- Case difficulty match: `[why this case is comparable to the paired case]`

## Evidence packet for both conditions

Record the identical facts that participants may use in either condition:

- SAS 2024 crop evidence: `[measure, positive count, valid denominator, share, and unit where confirmed]`
- Relevant sales or historical market context: `[channel, count, historical measure, or “not available”]`
- Known data-quality caveat: `[missingness, negative values, or price coverage]`
- Source status: `[provisional/approved as shown in the app]`

Use the same evidence packet for both methods. In the source-based condition, provide it in the format the organization currently uses. In the AgriLoss condition, ask the participant to find and review it in the dashboard.

## Local scenario information

Read this same fictional context to the participant in either condition:

> `[Describe only the local storage, collection, processor, buyer, seasonal-supply, or price conditions the task needs. Keep the facts identical between conditions and clearly label them as scenario information, not SAS survey evidence.]`

## Participant task prompt

> You are preparing a follow-up for `[fictional organization or role]` about `[crop]` in `[location]` for `[period]`. Using the information available, recommend the next investigation or analysis step. State what evidence supports your recommendation, what local information is still needed, who should collect it, and what should be checked before anyone considers an intervention or investment. Do not assume that sample values describe this location.
